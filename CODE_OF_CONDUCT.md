# Community code of conduct

We are building an open, evidence-led Linux NPU commons. Be respectful,
patient, and precise. Welcome beginners, credit prior and upstream work, share
reproduction details, and separate measured results from hypotheses.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, and
publishing another person's private data are not acceptable. Maintainers may
edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub Security
advisory form. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as practical
and respond proportionately and in good faith.
